In .NET, **Dependency Injection (DI)** is built directly into the framework. **C# 12 primary constructors** (`class Notifier(ISender sender)`) eliminate repetitive private field and constructor assignment boilerplate.

<Callout type="tip" title="Primary Constructors Keep DI Concise">
  <code>public class OrderService(IPaymentGateway gateway)</code> captures <code>gateway</code> across the entire class body with zero extra lines.
</Callout>
