When Docker, Kubernetes, or `systemd` restarts your Node.js server during a deploy, it sends **`SIGTERM`**. Listening for `process.on("SIGTERM", ...)` and calling `server.close()` lets in-flight HTTP requests finish cleanly instead of dropping connections mid-response.

<Callout type="tip" title="Structured JSON Logs Are Searchable">
  Logging JSON lines (<code>&#123;"level":"info","path":"/api/run","ms":42&#125;</code>) makes filtering and alerting effortless in journalctl, Datadog, or Grafana Loki.
</Callout>
