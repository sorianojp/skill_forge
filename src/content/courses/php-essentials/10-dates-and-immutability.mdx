Always choose **`DateTimeImmutable`** over the older mutable `DateTime`. Calling `->modify('+7 days')` returns a **brand-new** date object while leaving the original date untouched!

<Callout type="tip" title="Store UTC, Display Local">
  Store timestamps in UTC (<code>new DateTimeZone('UTC')</code>) in SQL databases, then convert to the user's timezone only when rendering.
</Callout>

## Try It Yourself

<PhpPlayground
  client:visible
  title="DateTimeImmutable & Intervals"
  code={`<?php
$start = new DateTimeImmutable('2026-11-26', new DateTimeZone('UTC'));
$nextWeek = $start->modify('+7 days');

echo "Start:     " . $start->format('M j, Y') . "\\n";
echo "Next Week: " . $nextWeek->format('M j, Y') . "\\n";
echo "ISO-8601:  " . $start->format(DateTimeInterface::ATOM) . "\\n";`}
