**SSH keys** replace brittle passwords with public-key cryptography. Only your **public key** (`~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub`) is added to the server's `~/.ssh/authorized_keys`; your private key stays locked down (`chmod 600`) locally. Once SSH works, **`rsync -avz --delete`** uploads only changed files in seconds.

<Callout type="warning" title="Mind the Trailing Slash in rsync">
  <code>rsync -avz dist/ user@host:/var/www/app/</code> syncs the <em>contents</em> of <code>dist</code> into <code>/var/www/app/</code>. Without the trailing slash, it creates <code>/var/www/app/dist/</code>.
</Callout>
