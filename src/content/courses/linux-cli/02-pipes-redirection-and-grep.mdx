The Unix philosophy is **"do one thing well, and compose tools with pipes (`|`)"**. Instead of writing a 50-line script, a one-line pipeline like `grep "ERROR" app.log | sort | uniq -c | wc -l` summarizes gigabytes of logs instantaneously.

<Callout type="tip" title="> Overwrites, >> Appends">
  <code>echo "line" &gt; log.txt</code> replaces the file; <code>echo "line" &gt;&gt; log.txt</code> appends to the end.
</Callout>
