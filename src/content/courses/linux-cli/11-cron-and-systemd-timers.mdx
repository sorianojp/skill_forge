A standard **cron** schedule has five fields: `MINUTE HOUR DAY_OF_MONTH MONTH DAY_OF_WEEK`:

- `0 3 * * *` -> Every day at 3:00 AM (great for nightly SQLite backups).
- `*/15 * * * *` -> Every 15 minutes.
- `0 9 * * 1` -> Every Monday at 9:00 AM.

<Callout type="tip" title="Always Use Absolute Paths in Cron">
  Cron runs with a minimal <code>PATH</code>. Write <code>/usr/bin/python3 /opt/scripts/backup.py</code> instead of assuming <code>python3</code> is on PATH.
</Callout>
