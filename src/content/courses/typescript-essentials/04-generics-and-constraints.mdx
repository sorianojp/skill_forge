**Generics** act like type-level parameters. Instead of losing type information with `any`, `function first<T>(items: T[]): T | undefined` remembers whether you passed `number[]` or `User[]` and returns the exact matching element type.

<Callout type="info" title="Type-Safe Property Access with keyof">
  <code>function pick&lt;T, K extends keyof T&gt;(obj: T, key: K): T[K]</code> guarantees at compile time that <code>key</code> actually exists on <code>obj</code>.
</Callout>
