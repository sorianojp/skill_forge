TypeScript **parameter properties** let you declare and assign a class field directly inside the constructor signature (`constructor(private readonly name: string)`), saving lines of repetitive boilerplate.

<Callout type="tip" title="implements Verifies Contracts at Compile Time">
  Adding <code>class ConsoleLogger implements Logger</code> forces TypeScript to check that every required method and signature is present.
</Callout>
