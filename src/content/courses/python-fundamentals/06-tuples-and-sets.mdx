**Tuples** (`(10, 20)`) are lightweight, immutable sequences ideal for fixed records and returning multiple values from a function. **Sets** (`{"HTML", "CSS"}`) store unique items with fast `in` membership checks and math-style operations:
- `a | b` (union: items in either set)
- `a & b` (intersection: items in both sets)
- `a - b` (difference: items in `a` but not `b`)
