Containers are **ephemeral**: when you remove a container (`docker rm`), any files written inside its container layer disappear. To persist database tables or uploaded files safely across container rebuilds, mount a **named Docker volume**.

<Callout type="info" title="Named Volumes vs. Bind Mounts">
  Use <strong>named volumes</strong> (<code>db_data:/var/lib/postgresql/data</code>) for production databases, and <strong>bind mounts</strong> (<code>./src:/app/src</code>) for local hot-reload development.
</Callout>
