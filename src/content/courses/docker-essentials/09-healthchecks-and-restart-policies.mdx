A container process can be "running" (`Up`) even while the app inside is deadlocked or failing requests. Adding a **`HEALTHCHECK`** tells Docker to periodically test `/api/health` and mark the container `healthy` or `unhealthy`. Pairing that with **`restart: unless-stopped`** ensures services bounce back automatically after a server reboot.

<Callout type="tip" title="Use depends_on with service_healthy">
  In Compose, <code>depends_on: db: condition: service_healthy</code> ensures your backend waits until Postgres is actually ready to accept queries, not just started.
</Callout>
