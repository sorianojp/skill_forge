Good schemas prevent bad data from ever entering your database:
- **`PRIMARY KEY`**: Uniquely identifies every row.
- **`NOT NULL`**: Forbids missing values.
- **`UNIQUE`**: Prevents duplicate emails or slugs.
- **`CHECK (...)`**: Enforces domain rules like `CHECK (score BETWEEN 0 AND 100)`.
- **`CREATE INDEX`**: Builds a fast B-tree lookup structure so `WHERE email = ?` stays instant even on millions of rows.

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't Over-Index Everything">
  Every index speeds up <code>SELECT</code> reads, but adds small overhead to <code>INSERT</code> and <code>UPDATE</code> writes. Index columns you actually filter or join on.
</Callout>
